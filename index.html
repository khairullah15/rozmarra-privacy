Rozmarra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Rozmarra keeps your records on your phone. We do not run a server for the app, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data. The app has no ads or analytics. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to send or share it.

1. Who we are

Rozmarra (Android package com.blazzer.rozmarra) is published by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. This policy applies only to the Rozmarra app.

2. Information stored on your device

The information below is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us.

  • Account details: your name, email address, optional phone number and a protected (hashed) form of your password. The account exists only on your device.
  • Khata records: names and phone numbers of people you add, amounts, dates, descriptions and reminders.
  • Cashbook records: income, expenses, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Group expenses: group names, members, amounts and settlements.
  • Notes: the notes you write.
  • Saved passwords and PINs: items you put in the Passwords section, encrypted using a key held by the Android Keystore on your phone.
  • App settings: preferences such as theme, currency, reminders, App Lock and a protected (hashed) balance PIN.

3. Device features and permissions

  • Fingerprint or device lock: used through Android to unlock the app. Rozmarra never receives or stores your fingerprint.
  • Notifications: used to show the reminders you turn on.
  • Contacts: Rozmarra does not have permission to read your contact list. If you tap Import from contacts, Android lets you pick one contact, and only that contact's name and number are given to the app.
  • Background and network status: Android's scheduling library uses wake lock, boot-completed and network-state permissions so reminders run on time. The app does not have the Android Internet permission.

4. When data leaves your device

Only when you take one of these actions:

  • Sending a balance message through WhatsApp or sharing it as text.
  • Exporting or sharing a PDF or Excel file.
  • Sending a backup by email or saving a backup file to a location you choose.

The app or service you choose receives that content, and its own privacy policy applies. We do not receive a copy.

5. Backups

Backups are created only when you ask. A backup protected with a password is encrypted. A backup without a password can be read by anyone who has the file, and saved passwords and PINs are included only in password-protected backups. Please keep your backup files and backup password safe, because we cannot recover them. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app.

6. Third parties, ads and analytics

Rozmarra does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking services. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties. Google Play may separately process information about your app download and purchases under Google's own policies.

7. Security

We use hashing for passwords and PINs, encryption for saved passwords and protected backups, and optional App Lock. No system is perfectly secure, so please also protect your phone with a screen lock.

8. Delete your account and data

Because your data is stored only on your phone, you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Open Rozmarra and go to Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm. This permanently erases that account and all of its records from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes the app's data from your phone.

We hold no copy, so deleted data cannot be restored unless you kept a backup. Any backup files you sent or saved elsewhere must be deleted from those places by you.

9. Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you edit it, delete it, delete your account or uninstall the app. Signing out does not delete it.

10. Children

Rozmarra is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
